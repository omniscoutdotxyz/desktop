# OmniScout Desktop 0.1.1

Fixes the macOS app signature so the download can open correctly.

On first launch, macOS may ask you to approve OmniScout in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**. Choose **Open Anyway**, then confirm **Open**.

Get OmniScout and follow the guided setup at [omniscout.xyz/desktop](https://omniscout.xyz/desktop).
